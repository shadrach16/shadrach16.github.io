How It Works

1

Authorize with TikTok

Log in through TikTok's official OAuth 2.0 flow. You grant publishing permission directly on TikTok's site — we never access your password.

2

Prepare Your Content

Create your photos or videos. AutoPilot Publisher handles formatting, captions, hashtags, and privacy settings for TikTok's API requirements.

3

Publish to TikTok

Content is uploaded through TikTok's official Content Posting API. Photo slideshows and videos are posted with full metadata and proper disclosures.

4

Track & Manage

Monitor published content status. All posts are tracked with publish timestamps and TikTok post IDs for your records.
