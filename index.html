Security

Your Data, Protected

Enterprise-grade security practices for your peace of mind

Encrypted Token Storage

OAuth tokens stored securely with encryption. Never exposed in logs, source code, or transmitted externally.

Zero Data Selling

We never sell, share, or transfer your data to third parties. Your information stays with you. Period.

Access tokens expire every 24 hours and are refreshed automatically — only when actively needed.

Remove access instantly from TikTok settings. All stored tokens are invalidated the moment you revoke.
