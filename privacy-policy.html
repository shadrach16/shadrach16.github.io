Privacy Policy — AutoPilot Publisher

Last updated: April 28, 2026

1. Introduction

AutoPilot Publisher ("the App") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights.

2. Data We Collect

When you authorize the App via TikTok OAuth, we receive and store:

DataPurposeRetention
TikTok Open IDIdentify your account for API callsUntil you revoke access
Access TokenPublish content on your behalfAuto-refreshed every 24 hours
Refresh TokenRenew access tokensUntil you revoke access (max 365 days)
Display Name & Avatar URLDisplay confirmation before publishingNot stored permanently

3. Data We Do NOT Collect

  • We do not collect your TikTok password.
  • We do not collect your personal information, email, phone number, or location.
  • We do not access your TikTok messages, followers list, or browsing history.
  • We do not sell, share, or transfer your data to any third parties.

4. How We Use Your Data

Your data is used solely to:

  • Authenticate with the TikTok Content Posting API.
  • Publish photo and video content to your TikTok account on your behalf.
  • Query your creator information (privacy level options) before posting.

5. Data Storage & Security

  • OAuth tokens are stored locally on the server in encrypted JSON files.
  • Tokens are never exposed in logs, source code, or transmitted to third parties.
  • Access tokens expire every 24 hours and are automatically refreshed.

6. Your Rights

You may at any time:

  • Revoke access: Go to TikTok → Settings → Security → Manage app permissions → Remove AutoPilot Publisher.
  • Request deletion: Contact us to delete all stored tokens and data.

Upon revocation, all stored tokens are invalidated immediately.

7. Third-Party Services

The App interacts only with the official TikTok Content Posting API (https://developers.tiktok.com). No other third-party services receive your data.

8. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy at any time. Changes will be reflected in the "Last updated" date above.

10. Contact

For privacy-related questions, contact: autopilotpublisher@proton.me
